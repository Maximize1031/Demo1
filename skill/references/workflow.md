# Workflow decisions and source handling

## Evidence

Every found row needs a filename plus heading or paragraph locator and a short supporting passage. Conflicts need at least two citations. Missing rows cite the reviewed scope and say “not in reviewed records,” not “client never sent.” Preserve document IDs and event times. Do not use invented confidence percentages.

An email stating a document is attached does not prove it was ingested. A call note can support client-reported facts but cannot replace an operating agreement or executed purchase agreement. Do not treat synthetic PDF props as real executed instruments. All demo outputs retain a visible Synthetic label.

## Requests

Write directly to the person who can answer. Use “property you’re selling,” “owner,” “person who can sign,” and “LLC operating agreement.” Avoid “relinquished,” “vesting,” and unexplained tax terminology in client email. Ask for each unresolved item once. Acknowledge specific documents already on file where helpful. Provide an easy fallback: “If escrow already has it, let me know and I’ll coordinate.”

Tax IDs and W-9s use an established secure channel. Do not invent a portal URL. Request coordination if no secure channel is known. Never quote SSNs/EINs in source excerpts, summaries, drafts, HTML, logs, or filenames.

An initial closing-date conflict goes to escrow. If a later email explicitly confirms the date and says the earlier date is superseded, resolve it and retain both earlier references in the change log. Do not ask the client to arbitrate escrow scheduling.

## Timing

This demo intentionally does not compute exchange deadlines from disputed or estimated closing dates. When actual transfer is confirmed in real work, use current IRS guidance: identification generally within 45 days; receipt generally by the earlier of 180 days or the applicable tax-return due date including extensions. Confirm tax-return timing and any applicable relief with the advisor before labeling a deadline final. Do not assume an estimated close has occurred or create calendar events automatically.

Source: [IRS Form 8824 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8824), checked October 15, 2026. This corrects the source skill's incomplete unconditional “closing + 180” wording.
